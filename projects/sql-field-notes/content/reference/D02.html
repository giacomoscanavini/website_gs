Method usage links

The method tables cover SQL and pandas syntax, behavior, and counterparts. Usage links identify executable syntax and recognized pandas aggregate names in the solutions. They do not match comments or narrative text.

Matches are a conservative source-code index, not a proof of correctness or exhaustive program analysis. A method may be present indirectly through a variable or custom helper without being detected. The original game dialects are preserved; MySQL labels apply to Leetcode submissions.
