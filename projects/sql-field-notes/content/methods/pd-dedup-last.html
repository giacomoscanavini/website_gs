Leetcode · pandas 1164 · Product price at a given date Study solution · line 6 
    valid = p.loc[p['change_date'].le(pd.Timestamp('2019-08-16'))]
    latest = valid.sort_values('change_date').drop_duplicates('product_id', keep='last')
    result = p[['product_id']].drop_duplicates().merge(latest[['product_id', 'new_price']], on='product_id', how='left')
    result['price'] = result['new_price'].fillna(10)
