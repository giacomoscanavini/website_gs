Cross-check the licence plate

Use the partial plate to find a second, independent candidate list.

SQL 
-- People associated to cars with plate matching the reported piece
SELECT 
	d.id, d.age, d.plate_number, d.car_make, d.car_model,
	p.name
FROM Drivers_license d
LEFT JOIN Person p ON d.id = p.license_id
WHERE 
	d.plate_number LIKE 'H42W%'
	OR d.plate_number LIKE '%H42W'
	OR d.plate_number LIKE '%H42W%'

How it works

Drivers_license is joined to Person through license_id. The partial plate H42W produces the saved list below, which can be compared with the gym candidates from the preceding step.

Pattern matching. The pattern %H42W% already includes matches at the beginning or end of the plate. pandas therefore needs one literal substring test. Neither query includes a gender filter.
