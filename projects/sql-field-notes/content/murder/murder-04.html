Follow the gym clue

Intersect the membership prefix, membership tier and visit date.

SQL 
/* Important information discovered: 
- Suspect seem a "man"
- Probably goes to the "Get Fit Now Gym" and has a gold memebership
- Bag membership number on bag starts with "48Z"
- Suspect got into a car with "H42W" contained in the plate

- The suspect has been recognized going to the same gym as Annabel
- The suspect was at the gym on January 9th 2018
*/

-- Gold membership at the gym on the reported day by Annabel
SELECT 
	g.membership_id, g.check_in_date,
	m.person_id, m.name, m.membership_status
FROM Get_fit_now_check_in g
LEFT JOIN Get_fit_now_member m ON g.membership_id = m.id
WHERE 
	g.check_in_date = '20180109' 
	AND m.membership_status = 'gold'
	AND m.id LIKE '48Z%'
pandas 
import pandas as pd

joined = get_fit_now_check_in.merge(get_fit_now_member, how='left',
                                   left_on='membership_id', right_on='id')
mask = (joined['check_in_date'].astype('string').eq('20180109')
        & joined['membership_status'].eq('gold')
        & joined['id'].str.startswith('48Z', na=False))
result = joined.loc[mask, ['membership_id', 'check_in_date', 'person_id',
                           'name', 'membership_status']].copy()

How it works

Get_fit_now_check_in contributes attendance; Get_fit_now_member contributes the person, name and membership tier. Filtering the joined records keeps gold members with IDs beginning 48Z who checked in on 9 January 2018.

Candidate evidence. These two rows match the gym clues, but the membership prefix alone does not identify the killer.
