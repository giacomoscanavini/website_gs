577 · Employee bonus

Return employee names and bonuses where the bonus is below 1000 or missing.

Pattern: Left join / NULL-aware filtering

Illustrative example

Ana has no bonus record, Bo has a bonus of 500 and Cleo has a bonus of 1000. Keep Ana with a missing bonus and Bo with 500; exclude Cleo.

My solution

SQL 
SELECT e.name, b.bonus
FROM Employee e
LEFT JOIN Bonus b ON e.empId = b.empId
WHERE b.bonus < 1000 OR b.bonus IS NULL
pandas 
import pandas as pd
def employee_bonus(employee: pd.DataFrame, bonus: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    df = employee.merge(bonus, on='empId', how='left')
    return df[(df.bonus < 1000) | (df.bonus.isna())][['name', 'bonus']]

Why it works

A left join preserves employees with no Bonus row. The OR condition includes those missing values along with bonuses strictly below 1000; a bonus equal to 1000 does not qualify.
