1068 · Product sales analysis I

Attach the product name to each sale and return product_name, year and price.

Pattern: Join / column selection

Illustrative example

Product 10 is a Keyboard. Sales for product 10 in 2024 at price 50 and in 2025 at price 55 become (Keyboard, 2024, 50) and (Keyboard, 2025, 55).

My solution

SQL 
SELECT p.product_name, s.year, s.price
FROM Sales s
JOIN Product p ON s.product_id = p.product_id
pandas 
import pandas as pd

def sales_analysis(sales: pd.DataFrame, product: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    df = sales.merge(product, on='product_id', how='inner')
    return df[['product_name', 'year', 'price']]

Why it works

The product_id join adds the name to each sales row without grouping the sales. Selecting three columns gives the requested output shape.

Each sale is matched to its product before selecting the requested columns.
