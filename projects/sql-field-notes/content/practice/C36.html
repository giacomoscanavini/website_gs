1527 · Patients with a disease

Keep patients with a space-delimited condition token beginning DIAB1.

Pattern: Token-prefix matching

Example

"DIAB100" and "ASTHMA DIAB100" qualify; "XDIAB100" does not.

Study solution

SQL 
SELECT patient_id, patient_name, conditions FROM Patients
WHERE conditions LIKE 'DIAB1%' OR conditions LIKE '% DIAB1%';
pandas 
import pandas as pd

def find_patients(patients: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    mask = patients['conditions'].str.contains(r'(?:^| )DIAB1', regex=True, na=False)
    return patients.loc[mask].copy()

Why it works

The prefix must begin either the whole string or a token after a space. It may be followed by more characters within the disease code.

Notes

DIAB1 must start a condition token: either at the beginning of the string or after a space.
