Early stopping as spectral regularization

Go beyond patience: understand which directions are learned early and why the stopping set is part of model selection.

The procedural definition

Evaluate a validation criterion during training, retain the best parameter state, and stop after a chosen number of evaluations without sufficient improvement. The guide uses

Vt<Vbest−δV_t<V_{\mathrm{best}}-\delta

as an improvement condition for a loss to be minimized. Patience protects against small noisy reversals; the minimum improvement δ\delta avoids treating every tiny change as progress.

The model retained should be the selected best checkpoint , not necessarily the state at the last iteration. Stopping and restoration are separate actions. The official Ignite handler demonstrates the stopping mechanism; the linked PyTorch transfer-learning tutorial demonstrates saving and restoring a best validation model.

Why restricting time can restrict complexity

The source describes early training as preferentially fitting stronger, better-identified directions, while later optimization can fit weaker or noisy components. Early stopping restricts the reachable solution along

θ0→θ1→⋯→θT.\theta_0\to\theta_1\to\cdots\to\theta_T.

That intuition becomes concrete in linear least squares. It should not be read as a guarantee that every neural network learns “simple” semantic features first. The derivation below states the setting in which the direction-filtering picture is explicit.

Expand the linear least-squares recurrence

Take

L(β)=12∥y−Xβ∥2,βt+1=(I−ηX⊤X)βt+ηX⊤y,L(\beta)=\frac12\lVert y-X\beta\rVert^2,\qquad \beta_{t+1}=(I-\eta X^\top X)\beta_t+\eta X^\top y,

with β0=0\beta_0=0 and constant step size. In a singular direction of X=UDV⊤X=UDV^\top , let ctc_t be the coefficient and aj=uj⊤ya_j=u_j^\top y . Then

ct+1=(1−ηdj2)ct+ηdjaj.c_{t+1}=(1-\eta d_j^2)c_t+\eta d_j a_j.

Summing the geometric series gives the source’s spectral expression for nonzero djd_j :

βt=Vdiag(1−(1−ηdj2)tdj)U⊤y.\beta_t=V\operatorname{diag}\left(\frac{1-(1-\eta d_j^2)^t}{d_j}\right)U^\top y.

Relative to OLS, the filter is

1−(1−ηdj2)t.1-(1-\eta d_j^2)^t.

With a suitably small nonoscillatory step, strong singular directions approach their fitted value quickly, while weak directions remain attenuated. For example, if ηdj2\eta d_j^2 is very small, the first several updates make only a small fraction of the eventual OLS adjustment.

Step by step Solve gradient descent in every singular direction, step by step

Setting. Use L(β)=∥y−Xβ∥2/2L(\beta)=\|y-X\beta\|^2/2 , constant step η>0\eta>0 , and β0=0\beta_0=0 . Let X=UrDrVr⊤X=U_rD_rV_r^\top contain only positive singular values djd_j . These assumptions are essential: a nonzero initialization leaves an additional term.

1 · Write the affine parameter recurrence

βt+1=(I−ηX⊤X)βt+ηX⊤y.\beta_{t+1}=(I-\eta X^\top X)\beta_t+\eta X^\top y.

The gradient is X⊤Xβt−X⊤yX^\top X\beta_t-X^\top y ; subtracting it gives the two terms above.

Let at=Vr⊤βta_t=V_r^\top\beta_t and c=Ur⊤yc=U_r^\top y . Multiplying by Vr⊤V_r^\top gives

at+1,j=(1−ηdj2)at,j+ηdjcj.a_{t+1,j}=(1-\eta d_j^2)a_{t,j}+\eta d_jc_j.

a1,j=ηdjcj,a2,j=ηdjcj(1+qj),at,j=ηdjcj∑s=0t−1qjs.a_{1,j}=\eta d_jc_j,\quad a_{2,j}=\eta d_jc_j(1+q_j),\quad a_{t,j}=\eta d_jc_j\sum_{s=0}^{t-1}q_j^s.

3 · Evaluate the geometric sum

Multiply S=1+q+⋯+qt−1S=1+q+\cdots+q^{t-1} by qq and subtract: (1−q)S=1−qt(1-q)S=1-q^t . Here 1−qj=ηdj21-q_j=\eta d_j^2 , so

at,j=ηdjcj1−qjtηdj2=1−(1−ηdj2)tdjcj.a_{t,j}=\eta d_jc_j\frac{1-q_j^t}{\eta d_j^2} =\frac{1-(1-\eta d_j^2)^t}{d_j}c_j.

Consequently

βt=Vrdiag(1−(1−ηdj2)tdj)Ur⊤y.\beta_t=V_r\operatorname{diag}\left(\frac{1-(1-\eta d_j^2)^t}{d_j}\right)U_r^\top y.

Zero-singular-value directions remain zero from this initialization; they must not be handled by literal division by zero.

4 · Distinguish stability from monotone shrinkage

Convergence in every represented direction requires |1−ηdj2|<1|1-\eta d_j^2|<1 , or 0<η<2/dmax20<\eta<2/d_{\max}^2 . To have 0≤qj<10\le q_j<1 and filter st(dj)=1−qjts_t(d_j)=1-q_j^t rise monotonically from zero toward one, the stronger bound 0<η≤1/dmax20<\eta\le1/d_{\max}^2 is sufficient. In the larger stable interval, a filter can oscillate above and below one.

5 · Compare with Ridge quantitatively

Ridge’s fitted-value factor is sλ(d)=d2/(d2+λ)s_\lambda(d)=d^2/(d^2+\lambda) . Matching one direction at a time would require

λj(t)=dj21−st(dj)st(dj)=dj2qjt1−qjt.\lambda_j(t)=d_j^2\frac{1-s_t(d_j)}{s_t(d_j)} =d_j^2\frac{q_j^t}{1-q_j^t}.

For t>0t>0 in the monotone regime this is nonnegative, but depends on jj . A single stopping time does not generally correspond to one common Ridge penalty. The shared idea is preferential suppression of weakly identified directions, not equality of estimators.

Compare with Ridge without equating them

Ridge’s relative filter is dj2/(dj2+λ)d_j^2/(d_j^2+\lambda) . Finite-time descent has the exponential-in-time filter above. Both suppress poorly identified directions, but their functional forms differ. There is not generally one λ\lambda that makes both procedures identical in every singular direction.

Expanded derivation assumption. Stability for the positive singular modes requires |1−ηdj2|<1|1-\eta d_j^2|<1 . A smaller condition, 0<ηdj2≤10<\eta d_j^2\le1 , makes the simple monotonic-filter intuition immediate. If the step is too large, oscillation or divergence invalidates the gentle “learn strong directions first” picture.

Validation is not untouched testing

The stopping criterion chooses a model. The validation set is consequently part of model selection. Repeatedly checking the final test set and choosing the stopping time from it makes that set part of selection too.

For temporal applications, the PDF requires a time-ordered validation block and any necessary embargo. A random split can provide an attractive curve while answering the wrong deployment question. Patience does not repair a leakage-prone split.

Practical interpretation

A very small patience can stop on noise; a very large patience weakens the restriction. Too much combined regularization can stop the model before useful structure is learned. Interpret the curve together with capacity, optimizer behavior, and the definition of the metric.

Check your understanding In what sense is early stopping related to Ridge?

In linear least squares from zero initialization, finite-time gradient descent filters singular directions. Weak directions are learned slowly and remain attenuated when training stops. Ridge also attenuates weak directions, but its filter is different, so the relationship is not a universal equality with one λ.
