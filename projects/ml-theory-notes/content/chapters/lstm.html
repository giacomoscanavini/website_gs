Two states with different roles

An LSTM introduces a cell state ctc_t in addition to the hidden output hth_t . The cell provides an additive route for storing and modifying information. The hidden state exposes a gated transformation of that cell to the rest of the network.

For concatenated input ut=[ht−1;xt]u_t=[h_{t-1};x_t] , the source defines

ft=σ(Wfut+bf),it=σ(Wiut+bi),f_t=\sigma(W_fu_t+b_f),\qquad i_t=\sigma(W_iu_t+b_i),
c̃t=tanh(Wcut+bc),ct=ft⊙ct−1+it⊙c̃t,\tilde c_t=\tanh(W_cu_t+b_c),\qquad c_t=f_t\odot c_{t-1}+i_t\odot\tilde c_t,
ot=σ(Wout+bo),ht=ot⊙tanh(ct).o_t=\sigma(W_ou_t+b_o),\qquad h_t=o_t\odot\tanh(c_t).

The forget gate retains or attenuates old cell content. The input gate controls new candidate content. The output gate controls how much transformed cell content is exposed as the hidden state. These sigmoid-valued gates are continuous controls, not discrete instructions or class probabilities.

Why the additive path matters

A vanilla recurrent state repeatedly passes through a full nonlinear transformation. The LSTM cell instead contains a direct additive term from the previous cell. For a standard cell without peepholes, hold the previous hidden-state coordinate ht−1h_{t-1} fixed while differentiating the cell-state coordinate. The direct block is

∂ct∂ct−1|ht−1fixed=diag(ft).\left.\frac{\partial c_t}{\partial c_{t-1}}\right|_{h_{t-1}\ \mathrm{fixed}}=\operatorname{diag}(f_t).

Over several steps, that particular direct path scales coordinatewise as a product of the realized forget gates. When those values stay close to one, the path can retain useful sensitivity rather than repeatedly crush it through saturation.

The qualification is essential: the total derivative contains additional paths because the gates depend on recurrent variables. The source’s approximation isolates the memory-path intuition; the block form above makes that scope precise. It is not the full Jacobian of the joint cell and hidden state.

Step by step Derive the direct cell-state path and the full state Jacobian

Setting. Consider a standard LSTM without peephole connections. At a fixed input, gates f,i,of,i,o and candidate gg depend on the previous hidden state hh , while

c′=f(h)⊙c+i(h)⊙g(h),h′=o(h)⊙tanh(c′).c'=f(h)\odot c+i(h)\odot g(h),\qquad h'=o(h)\odot\tanh(c').

Treat previous cell cc and hidden state hh as independent coordinates of the recurrent state s=(c,h)s=(c,h) when forming its local Jacobian.

1 · Hold the hidden-state coordinate fixed

If hh is fixed, the gate values do not change when cc changes. For coordinates j,kj,k ,

∂c′j∂ck=fj𝟏j=k.\frac{\partial c'_j}{\partial c_k}=f_j\,\mathbf1_{j=k}.

Therefore the direct cell-to-cell block is F=diag(f)F=\operatorname{diag}(f) , not the vector ff itself.

2 · Include the routes through the gates

Let Jf=∂f/∂hJ_f=\partial f/\partial h , with analogous definitions for i,g,oi,g,o . The product rule gives

A=∂c′∂h=diag(c)Jf+diag(g)Ji+diag(i)Jg.A=\frac{\partial c'}{\partial h} =\operatorname{diag}(c)J_f+\operatorname{diag}(g)J_i+\operatorname{diag}(i)J_g.

These terms exist because changing the previous hidden state changes forgetting, writing, and candidate content.

3 · Differentiate the exposed hidden state

Let T=diag(o⊙[1−tanh2(c′)])T=\operatorname{diag}(o\odot[1-\tanh^2(c')]) . Then

∂h′∂c=TF,\frac{\partial h'}{\partial c}=TF,
∂h′∂h=TA+diag(tanh(c′))Jo.\frac{\partial h'}{\partial h}=TA+\operatorname{diag}(\tanh(c'))J_o.

The first contribution passes through the updated cell; the second passes directly through the output gate.

4 · Assemble the full transition Jacobian

∂(c′,h′)∂(c,h)=[FATFTA+diag(tanh(c′))Jo].\frac{\partial(c',h')}{\partial(c,h)}= \begin{bmatrix} F&A\\ TF&TA+\operatorname{diag}(\tanh(c'))J_o \end{bmatrix}.

Across time, gradients involve products of these full block matrices. The product of forget gates describes one additive cell path, not the entire derivative of the recurrent computation.

5 · Interpret the benefit without overclaiming

Along the direct path, factors near one can preserve sensitivity without repeatedly multiplying by an arbitrary recurrent matrix and a tanh derivative. This eases a particular source of vanishing gradients. Other paths, saturation, gate dynamics, and optimization still matter, so an LSTM does not guarantee nonvanishing total gradients or unlimited memory.

Read a memory timescale from the gate

For an approximately constant scalar forget gate ff , old content contributes proportionally to fkf^k after kk steps, before new content and other paths are considered. A value of 0.990.99 decays far more slowly than 0.50.5 .

Different hidden coordinates can learn different gate values and therefore different effective timescales. This is more informative than saying an LSTM “remembers long-term information”: it learns what to retain, overwrite, and expose, coordinate by coordinate and step by step.

Parameter counts and library conventions

With input width dxd_x and hidden width dhd_h , the PDF’s single-bias concatenated formulation has approximately

4dh(dx+dh+1)4d_h(d_x+d_h+1)

parameters before output layers. There are four affine maps: three gates plus a candidate transformation.

Official PyTorch supplement. A standard unprojected single-layer, single-direction nn.LSTM stores separate input and recurrent biases, giving 4dh(dx+dh+2)4d_h(d_x+d_h+2) parameters with biases enabled. This is a storage/parameterization distinction from the source’s formula, not an unexplained change to it. Stacking, bidirectionality, and projections require their own counts.

Step by step Count recurrent weights and distinguish one bias from two

Setting. A single unidirectional, unprojected LSTM layer has input width dd and hidden width hh . It computes four affine transformations: input, forget, candidate, and output.

1 · Count one transformation in the mathematical convention

A transformation of concatenated [ht−1;xt]∈ℝh+d[h_{t-1};x_t]\in\mathbb R^{h+d} to hh outputs has h(h+d)h(h+d) weights and hh biases. Four transformations therefore have

4[h(h+d)+h]=4h(d+h+1)4[h(h+d)+h]=4h(d+h+1)

parameters in the single-bias convention used by the source.

2 · Count the tensors in the PyTorch convention

For one layer, the input weight tensor has shape (4h,d)(4h,d) and contains 4hd4hd values. The recurrent weight tensor has shape (4h,h)(4h,h) and contains 4h24h^2 values. With biases enabled, two bias vectors each have length 4h4h . Hence

N=4hd+4h2+8h=4h(d+h+2).N=4hd+4h^2+8h=4h(d+h+2).

Adding the two bias vectors is functionally equivalent to one effective bias, but the library stores two trainable tensors.

3 · Verify a small example

For d=3,h=5d=3,h=5 , the four counts are 6060 , 100100 , 2020 , and 2020 , totaling 200200 . The single-bias mathematical count is 4(5)(3+5+1)=1804(5)(3+5+1)=180 . The difference is the additional 2020 -element bias vector.

4 · State what is outside this formula

An output head adds its own parameters. Stacked layers receive the previous layer’s output width; bidirectionality changes that width and adds a second direction; an LSTM projection changes recurrent shapes and adds projection weights. Sequence length and batch size do not multiply the number of shared parameters. The vanilla RNN and GRU analogues replace four affine blocks with one and three, respectively, under the same assumptions.

Where the architecture is appropriate

The guide associates LSTMs with moderate-length sequences, forecasting, biosignals, speech, and streaming settings. Bidirectional variants use future as well as past context and are inappropriate when that future information would be unavailable at inference.

The official sequence-model tutorial includes an LSTM part-of-speech tagger. The time-sequence example includes a prediction plot. Use those existing implementations to examine state shapes and training, while keeping the architecture’s central question in view: which information must persist, for how long, and under what prediction-time constraints?

Check your understanding Why does the LSTM cell state help with long-range gradients?

Its additive update contains a direct path scaled by the forget gate. Holding gate dependencies aside, the derivative along that path is the product of forget gates. Values near one can preserve sensitivity longer, but the full derivative includes other paths and useful memory is not guaranteed.
