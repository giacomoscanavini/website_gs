Physics

Neutrino physics

My physics work has centered on neutrino-argon scattering and liquid-argon time projection chamber detectors. The main themes are cross-section measurements, neutral-current pion production, charged-current topologies, reconstruction studies, scintillation and purity monitoring, and detector performance across the MicroBooNE, SBND, ArgoNeuT, and DUNE experimental programs.

Neuroscience

Cognitive signals

My neuroscience and cognitive-signal work focuses on EEG-based measurement, artifact removal, event synchronization, pupil-linked attention markers, covert cognition during natural listening, and high-frequency neural signatures. The common thread is building reliable signal-processing methods for human cognitive assessment and neuroengineering systems.
